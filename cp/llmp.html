LLM Python Engineer

Train, evaluate, and optimize AI models with Python, PyTorch, and modern LLM workflows including SFT and RLHF. View all openings

About the Role

  • Develop efficient, high-quality code to train and optimize artificial intelligence models
  • Evaluate and benchmark model performance and analyze results to improve the model
  • Write comprehensive, logical explanations for feedback with technical expertise
  • Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), including creating and using task-specific datasets
  • Collaborate with engineers on Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF) to refine models
  • Create and refine optimal responses to improve AI performance, emphasizing clarity, relevance, and technical accuracy
  • Write efficient code, conduct peer reviews, and maintain documentation
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams
  • Explore and integrate new tools and techniques to enhance AI training processes

Skills Required

  • 2+ years of experience as a Python Engineer
  • 1+ years of experience with Python frameworks for machine learning
  • TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Scikit-learn experience is a plus
  • Experience implementing machine learning models
  • Supervised and unsupervised learning algorithms (e.g., SVM, Decision Trees, Random Forest, k-NN)
  • Knowledge of CNNs, RNNs, GANs, and transfer learning
  • Good to have: Keras, Pandas/Numpy

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working in agile/lean teams and fast-paced environments
  • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code tools
  • Prior experience in CRM or SaaS applications is a plus
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills

What We Offer

  • Opportunity to work on impactful projects with room for innovation
  • Flexible work environment with a culture focused on learning and growth
  • Competitive salary and benefits package

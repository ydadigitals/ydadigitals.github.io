Website Creator & SEO Specialist Intern

Shape our web presence — create pages, optimize content for search, and help more businesses discover ydadigitals consulting and CRM products. View all openings

About the Role

We are looking for a Website Creator and SEO Specialist Intern to help grow our digital footprint. You will work with marketing and engineering to publish web content, improve on-page SEO, and ensure our site reflects the quality of our CRM solution and consulting services. This is an ideal role for someone eager to learn how modern websites are built, maintained, and discovered through search.

Responsibilities

  • Create and update website pages that align with our brand, tone, and user experience standards
  • Write and refine web copy for clarity, conversion, and search performance
  • Implement on-page SEO — meta titles and descriptions, headings, internal links, and structured data
  • Conduct keyword research and recommend content improvements based on search intent
  • Track site analytics and search performance; summarize insights for the team
  • Collaborate with engineering on site updates, technical SEO, and accessibility fixes
  • Support content and visual assets that strengthen our web and marketing campaigns
  • Stay current with SEO best practices, web standards, and content quality guidelines

Things You Will Learn

  • HTML and CSS fundamentals for structuring and styling web content
  • On-page and technical SEO principles for static and modern web sites
  • Google Search Console and Analytics for measuring traffic and discoverability
  • Keyword research, content optimization, and search-intent mapping
  • Responsive design and accessibility basics for inclusive web experiences
  • Content workflows — drafting, review, publishing, and iteration
  • How engineering and marketing collaborate on a production website

Good to Have

  • Familiarity with static sites, GitHub Pages, or similar publishing workflows
  • Basic understanding of web performance and Core Web Vitals
  • Portfolio of writing, design, or personal web projects
  • Interest in B2B SaaS, consulting, or CRM products
  • Strong communication, attention to detail, and teamwork skills

What We Offer

  • Hands-on experience shaping a real company website and search presence
  • Flexible work environment with a culture focused on learning and growth
  • Mentorship from engineering and marketing collaborators

How to Apply

Send your resume, a brief note on why you are interested in web and SEO, and any portfolio links to hello@ydadigitals.com.
